Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Macro Headshots ("we", "us", "our"), a professional headshot studio at 195 US-46, Suite 9, Totowa, New Jersey 07512, collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website, contact us or book a session.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill in our appointment form we collect your first and last name, phone number, email address, preferred session date, the package you are interested in and your message. If you call us, we may note your name and contact details to arrange your session.
  • Photographs. If you book a session, we take and store the photographs from your session in order to retouch and deliver them to you.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically receives basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, which is used to deliver and secure the site.
  • Cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details of cookies and similar technologies used on this site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule, confirm or change your appointment.
  • To carry out your session, and to retouch and deliver your photographs.
  • To communicate with you about your booking, including reminders and follow-ups.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • HighLevel / LeadConnector, which provides our appointment form and customer management system.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts used on this site.
  • Instagram (Meta), which loads only if you accept cookies.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, property or safety.

Photographs and your consent

Your photographs belong in your hands. We will not use your photographs in our portfolio, website or social media without your permission. If you have given permission and later change your mind, contact us and we will stop using them in new materials.

How long we keep information

We keep booking information and session photographs only as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and accounting requirements, after which we delete or anonymize it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you messages. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. To make a request, call us at 877-622-4973 or write to us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. Sessions for minors are booked by a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
