Where can I get LinkedIn headshots near Prospect Park, NJ?+

Prospect Park clients come to our studio at 195 US-46, Suite 9, Totowa, New Jersey 07512. From Prospect Park, head southwest through Haledon or Paterson to US-46 in Totowa — our studio is on Route 46. A LinkedIn headshot is $50 for a 5-minute session, retouched and emailed within 24 hours.

How much does a headshot cost?+

Packages start at $50 for one headshot from a 5-minute session, up to $250 for 10 headshots from a 1-hour session.

Can small-business owners get branding photos?+

Yes — our personal branding sessions are ideal for owners who want photos for their website, social media and marketing.

What is the turnaround time?+

We deliver all retouched pictures within 24 hours.

How many backgrounds can I choose from?+

Unlimited. We shoot on studio backdrops and can change your background digitally to the color you want.

Are all photos retouched?+

Yes. Every headshot you choose is edited, retouched and color graded.

How long is a session and how many photos do you take?+

1 headshot ($50): a 5-minute session where we take about 5–10 photos in different poses and you pick 1.

3 headshots ($100): a 10–15 minute session with about 15–20 photos, and you pick 3.

5 headshots ($150): a 30-minute session with about 20–40 photos, and you pick 5.

10 headshots ($250): a 1-hour session with about 40–100 photos, and you pick 10.

How do I receive my photos? Do you print them?+

Your retouched, color-graded headshots are emailed to you as digital files. All images are digital only — we do not offer printing.
