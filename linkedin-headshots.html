How much is a LinkedIn headshot?+

Our single-headshot package is $50 for a 5-minute session, retouched and emailed within 24 hours.

What size photo does LinkedIn need?+

We deliver a high-resolution file that you can upload directly; LinkedIn crops it to a circle, so we frame your face in the center.

Are professional headshots worth it for LinkedIn?

For most professionals, yes. LinkedIn has reported that members with a profile photo get up to 21 times more profile views and 9 times more connection requests, and a professional headshot makes that first impression a strong one.

What should I wear for a corporate headshot?

Wear well-fitted business attire in solid, mid-to-dark colors, avoid busy patterns and logos, and bring a second top or jacket if your package allows more than one look.

How much do professional headshots cost in New Jersey?

Headshot prices depend mostly on session length and how many retouched images you receive. At our Totowa studio, packages run from $50 for one retouched headshot to $250 for ten.
