How much do professional headshots cost in New Jersey?

Short answer: Headshot prices depend mostly on session length and how many retouched images you receive. At our Totowa studio, packages run from $50 for one retouched headshot to $250 for ten.

Headshot prices in New Jersey vary widely because photographers bundle different things into a "session". Before comparing quotes, check what is actually included.

What changes the price

  • Number of final, retouched images. This is the biggest driver. Some studios include one image and charge for each extra; others include several.
  • Session length. A quick 5-minute session is enough for one strong LinkedIn photo. More time means more outfits, poses and backgrounds.
  • Retouching. Check whether retouching is included or billed per image.
  • Location. Studio sessions usually cost less than having a photographer travel to your office.
  • Turnaround. Some photographers deliver in a week or more; faster delivery can cost extra.
  • Extras. Hair and makeup, printed copies or usage licensing can add to the total.

What a typical New Jersey session costs

A HeadshotPro survey of 751 photographer websites (data updated January 2025) put the median New Jersey headshot session at $250, and found that 66% of packages include three or fewer headshots. Nationally, Thumbtack estimates about $304 per session. At our studio, $250 buys ten retouched headshots from a 1-hour session.
