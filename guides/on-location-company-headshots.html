Headshot guide

Can an entire company book headshots on location?

Short answer: Yes, for larger teams. We bring our studio equipment to your office or location within 30 miles of our Totowa studio, with a minimum of 10 employees. Call 877-622-4973 for a quote.

Yes, for larger teams. If your company is within 30 miles of our Totowa studio and has at least 10 employees to photograph, we bring our studio equipment to your office or location. We set up in a conference room or open space and photograph employees one after another, so nobody loses more than a few minutes of their day.

Why companies choose on-location headshots

  • Consistency: the same lighting, framing and background for every person, so your website and directory look uniform.
  • Convenience: no travel for employees; sessions fit between meetings.
  • Speed: retouched images are emailed within 24 hours.
  • Flexibility: studio-style backdrops or natural office settings, matched to your brand.

How it works

  1. Plan: tell us how many people, your location and the look you want.
  2. Schedule: we share a time plan so each person knows when to step in.
  3. Shoot: each person gets a few minutes and picks their favorite.
  4. Deliver: retouched, color-graded images arrive by email within 24 hours.

Prefer the studio?

Teams of fewer than 10, or companies more than 30 miles away, are welcome at our Totowa studio on US-46. Either way, call 877-622-4973 to plan your company's session and get a quote.
