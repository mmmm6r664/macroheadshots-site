Headshot guide

Can I get professionally retouched headshots within 24 hours?

Short answer: Yes. Every headshot you choose is edited, retouched, color graded and emailed to you within 24 hours of your session.

Yes. Fast turnaround is standard at Macro Headshots, not an add-on. Every headshot you choose is edited, retouched and color graded, then emailed to you within 24 hours of your session.

How it works

  1. Shoot. We take photos in different poses and expressions during your session.
  2. Pick. You choose your favorites, as many as your package includes (1, 3, 5 or 10).
  3. Retouch. We remove blemishes, even out skin tone and refine details while keeping you looking natural.
  4. Deliver. Your finished digital files arrive by email within 24 hours.

When fast delivery matters

  • A job application or interview this week.
  • A new role announcement or company website update.
  • Medical residency (ERAS) or graduate school deadlines.
  • A speaking event, press release or award submission.

Tips to keep it fast

  • Book a time and arrive ready: outfit pressed, hair done.
  • Decide on your favorites before you leave the studio.
  • Tell us where the photo will be used, so we can deliver a suitable crop.

Common questions

Does 24-hour delivery cost extra?+

No. Delivery within 24 hours is included with every package.

How are photos delivered?+

Your retouched headshots are emailed to you as digital files.
