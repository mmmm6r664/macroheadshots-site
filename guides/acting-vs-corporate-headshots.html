What is the difference between acting and corporate headshots?

Short answer: Corporate headshots aim to look polished and trustworthy for business use. Acting headshots aim to show your true look and range so casting directors can picture you in a role, with lighter retouching and more varied expressions.

Both are portraits from the chest up, but they do different jobs and are judged by different people.

Corporate headshotActing headshot
PurposeLinkedIn, company website, biosCasting submissions and agency profiles
AudienceRecruiters, clients, colleaguesCasting directors and agents
ExpressionApproachable, confident, consistentVaried: show range and personality
WardrobeBusiness attireSimple, character-suggestive clothing
BackgroundClean, often brand-matchedNeutral, never distracting
RetouchingPolishedLight: must look exactly like you

Tips for acting headshots

  • Bring two or three tops that suggest the roles you are right for.
  • Keep makeup natural; casting wants to see the person who will walk in.
  • Book a longer session (30 minutes or 1 hour) to capture different looks.

Tips for corporate headshots

  • Match your company's style if teammates already have headshots.
  • Choose a background that works on both light and dark website designs.
  • One great image is often enough; the 5-minute session suits most people.
