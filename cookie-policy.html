Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Macro Headshots website uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage), and how you can control them. For more about how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, like local storage, work in a comparable way. They can be set by the website you visit (first-party) or by other services that site uses (third-party).
